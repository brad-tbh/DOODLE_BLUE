About Campus Events

Campus Events is a student-centric platform that organises and promotes a wide range of events including technical, sport and cultural activities. Our goal is to encourage participation, creativity and collaboration among students.

Our Mission

💻 Engage Students

Provide opportunities to explore, learn and showcase talents.

👥 Build Community

Connect students across departments and interests.

🏆 Create Opportunities

Organise meaningful events and experiences throughout the year.

Our Event Types

💻 Technical

Hackathons, tech talks and innovation events.

🎵 Cultural

Music, dance, art and literary events.

🏆 Sports

Intra and inter-college competitions.

👥 Workshops

Skill development and industry sessions.
